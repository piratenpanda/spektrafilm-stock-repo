Spectral upsampling

darktable turns each pixel’s colour into a spectrum before the film sees it, through a spectral upsampling table. The pack can carry one table or both; the module lists every table it carries, and an edit records the one it renders with.

Install

The pack installs beside the packs darktable downloads, in a folder named after its pack hash, and one line in darktablerc makes it the pack new edits use. Edits that already record a pack keep theirs.

  1. Download the pack

    Pick your stocks and tables above and press Download pack. You get <pack hash>.zip with one folder of the same name inside, holding pack.json, the spectral tables and profiles.

  2. Unzip it into darktable’s packs folder

    Unzip into ~/.local/share/darktable/spektrafilm/packs/ so you end up with packs/<pack hash>/pack.json. Keep the folder name: darktable checks it against the pack hash inside. Nothing else in that folder needs to move.

  3. Make it the default pack

    Close darktable, then add this line to ~/.config/darktable/darktablerc. The hash is this pack’s own and follows your selection. This step is not optional: darktable only uses a pack in that folder that its own data repository does not publish while this line names it, and otherwise ignores the folder. To go back to darktable’s own pack for new edits, delete the line; edits you made with this pack keep using it as long as its folder stays.

    Select at least one film to get the line.

  4. Start darktable

    Open the spektrafilm module. Your stocks are in the film and paper lists, and the tables you chose in its spectral upsampling list. darktable’s built-in spektrafilm looks are offered only with its own published packs, so they do not appear while this pack is the default.

Questions

Where do the stocks come from?

darktable’s own data pack (darktable-spektrafilm) is the base. Every stock from Negs that darktable does not have is added with negs_to_darktable_pack.py: B&W profiles are widened to three channels, the exposure grid is snapped to the pack’s, and grain, couplers, halation and print filters are filled in from Negs’ presets. Nothing darktable already defines is changed.

How often is it updated?

A scheduled job pulls both repositories every night and rebuilds the catalogue. If a merge fails, the site keeps the last build that worked. The date in the header is the last good sync; the build log has the details.

Can I install more than one pack from here?

Yes, each in its own folder under packs/. New edits use the one default_pack names. An edit remembers the pack it was made with and keeps finding it while its folder is there, whichever pack is the default; delete the folder and the edit falls back to another pack with a warning that it was developed with a different one.

Will my existing edits look different?

Not for darktable’s own stocks: their profiles and the spectral table are copied unchanged. When an added paper would change which paper one of darktable’s films picks on its own, that film is pinned to the paper it used before. The build log lists every film this applies to.

What does “approximate” mean?

The profile was not transcribed from a manufacturer’s datasheet. Most of these are Fotufilm profiles converted for Negs; they render well but are fitted rather than measured.

Why are some papers added on their own?

darktable prints every film on a paper. Each film names one, or darktable picks the first suitable paper in the pack. The pack creator always includes the paper each selected film would use with the whole catalogue, so a smaller pack renders the same as the full one.

Can I share or sell the pack?

The profiles are CC BY-SA 4.0. You can share and adapt them if you credit the authors, keep the licence and note your changes; every profile in the zip carries its author and licence in its metadata block, and the full licence texts are linked under Sources. Pass those on if you share the pack. The spektrafilm author asks that the data is not resold.

Which spectral upsampling table should I pick?

hanatos2025 is the table darktable’s pack has always shipped and the one every existing edit was made with. arctic2026beta04 is a reflectance table recovered under D65, and a beta: it renders differently, not better. Keep both in the pack to switch per edit in the module. Making arctic the table new edits start with also changes edits that never chose a table, and darktable then warns that they were developed with a different one.
