Install

darktable prefers a pack you install by hand over the ones it downloads, so this pack takes over as soon as darktable restarts.

  1. Download the pack

    Pick your stocks above and press Download pack. You get one zip with a spektrafilm folder inside.

  2. Put the folder in darktable’s data folder

    Unzip into ~/.local/share/darktable/ so you end up with ~/.local/share/darktable/spektrafilm/pack.json. If a spektrafilm folder with a pack.json is already there, move it somewhere safe first. Leave the packs folder alone: it holds darktable’s downloads.

  3. Restart darktable

    Open the spektrafilm module. Your stocks are in the film and paper lists. To go back to darktable’s own pack, delete pack.json, spectra_lut.f32 and profiles from that folder and restart.

Questions

Where do the stocks come from?

darktable’s own data pack (darktable-spektrafilm) is the base. Every stock from Negs that darktable does not have is added with negs_to_darktable_pack.py: B&W profiles are widened to three channels, the exposure grid is snapped to the pack’s, and grain, couplers, halation and print filters are filled in from Negs’ presets. Nothing darktable already defines is changed.

How often is it updated?

A scheduled job pulls both repositories every night and rebuilds the catalogue. If a merge fails, the site keeps the last build that worked. The date in the header is the last good sync; the build log has the details.

Will my existing edits look different?

Not for darktable’s own stocks: their profiles and the spectral table are copied unchanged. When an added paper would change which paper one of darktable’s films picks on its own, that film is pinned to the paper it used before. The build log lists every film this applies to.

What does “approximate” mean?

The profile was not transcribed from a manufacturer’s datasheet. Most of these are Fotufilm profiles converted for Negs; they render well but are fitted rather than measured.

Why are some papers added on their own?

darktable prints every film on a paper. Each film names one, or darktable picks the first suitable paper in the pack. Lightbox always includes the paper each selected film would use with the whole catalogue, so a smaller pack renders the same as the full one.

Can I share or sell the pack?

The profiles are CC BY-SA 4.0. You can share and adapt them if you credit the authors, keep the licence and note your changes; the zip carries the licences and a credit list for exactly what you picked. The spektrafilm author asks that the data is not resold.
